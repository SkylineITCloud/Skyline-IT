# Delivery and fulfilment supplier shortlist

25 October 2026  |  Operational reference

## Recommended starting point

Request comparable quotes from Bob Go, Pargo and one direct courier. Consider Parcelninja or DSV once volumes justify outsourced warehousing. This is a researched shortlist, not an exhaustive ranking or proof of delivery quality. No supplier has been appointed or contacted.

## Shipping platforms

Bob Go provides access to multiple couriers, including The Courier Guy, RAM, Internet Express, Pargo, Fastway, SkyNet, Citi Sprint and MTE Xpress. Ask about current platform fees, returns charges and insurance exclusions. Source: https://www.bobgo.co.za/faqs

Shiplogic supplies courier operations software and integration tooling. It does not by itself replace a courier or warehouse agreement. Source: https://www.shiplogic.com/platform

## Direct courier candidates

DPD South Africa documents APIs for waybills, collections, labels and shipment updates. Request domestic rates, remote-area charges, claims limits and returns terms. Source: https://www.dpd.com/za/en/contact-and-support/integration-options/

Internet Express documents e-commerce integration through Shiplogic. Ask for Durban collection cutoffs and approved routes for installed lithium batteries. Source: https://www.internetexpress.co.za/about-us/website-integration

The Courier Guy is available through Bob Go and its own shipping tools. Obtain written electronics acceptance, battery restrictions and packaging/cover conditions. Sources: https://www.bobgo.co.za/faqs and https://www.bobgo.co.za/insurance

## Pickup points and returns

Pargo offers click and collect and customer returns via participating pickup points, a returns URL, portal or API. Confirm which points support returns, battery acceptance and intended customer postcodes. Sources: https://pargo.co.za/business-solutions/returns/ and https://pargo.co.za/business-solutions/click-and-collect/

Bob Box provides a locker network for sending, collection and returns through courier partners. Check courier availability, locker size, collection windows and Durban coverage. Sources: https://www.bobbox.co.za/ and https://www.bobbox.co.za/locations

PAXI provides store-based parcel delivery and business options. Check bag limits, battery acceptance and collection requirements. Source: https://www.paxi.co.za/paxi-for-you

## Warehousing and fulfilment

Parcelninja advertises e-commerce warehousing, fulfilment, distribution and reporting. Request minimum fees, receiving, storage, serial-number capture, pick-and-pack and returns inspection. Source: https://www.parcelninja.com/

DSV offers South African e-commerce and contract logistics. Compare onboarding, minimum volumes, stock visibility and returns handling. Source: https://www.dsv.com/en-za/our-solutions/contract-logistics/ecommerce-solutions

## Selection criteria

Compare total cost per packed parcel, intended postcode coverage, loss/damage cover, battery acceptance, returns costs and support. Confirm current rates directly. A courier transit estimate starts after handover and does not describe the manufacturing or pre-order waiting period.

## Supplier quotation request

Please quote for Mzansi Connect Pendant, Wristband and Clip pre-orders dispatched from Durban. Each contains a rechargeable lithium battery. We will supply final battery watt-hours, installed-battery details, relevant test documents, packed dimensions, weight, value and monthly parcel forecast before contracting.

Itemise collection, delivery, customer returns, return-to-sender, remote areas, fuel, VAT, cover and failed delivery. Quote one, two and three devices per parcel for metro, regional and rural routes.

## Battery acceptance

Confirm installed lithium battery acceptance in writing, ground versus air restrictions, packaging, test-summary requirements and damaged-battery procedures. Damaged, swollen or overheating batteries must not enter an ordinary returns route without approved handling instructions.

## Warehouse quotation

Include receiving, storage, serial-number capture, stock reconciliation, pick-and-pack, dispatch cutoffs, branded inserts, returns quarantine and inspection, minimum commitments and stock retrieval on termination.

## Privacy and accountability

Only share data needed for delivery or return. Do not disclose SOS history, trusted contacts or location history. Request data-processing terms and retention rules. Use discreet packaging and customer-approved notifications. Mzansi Connect remains responsible for customer refund handling even when a third party carries the parcel.

## Decision record

Founder to record three written quotes, battery acceptance, intended postcode coverage, all-in cost and the returns workflow. No shipping rate or supplier performance promise has been adopted in this document.
