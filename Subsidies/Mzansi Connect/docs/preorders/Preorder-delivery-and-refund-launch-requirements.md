# Preorder delivery and refund launch requirements

25 October 2026  |  Commercial launch requirements

## Purpose

This completed internal specification identifies the requirements for customer terms covering Pendant, Wristband and Clip pre-orders. Confirm the legal seller, dispatch dates, shipping charges and USD schedule before issuing a customer agreement. Statutory rights prevail. This specification does not activate payments.

## Seller and preorder details

Brand: Mzansi Connect, a Skyline IT company. Support: projectpstg@gmail.com. Insert the contracting legal entity, registration details where applicable, physical service address, telephone and applicable VAT status.

A pre-order is not ready for immediate dispatch. Each confirmation must specify design, quantity, accessories, tested capabilities, service coverage, expected dispatch window and estimated transit period. Design illustrations do not prove product performance.

## Pricing

Device instalments are R350 per device per month for six months, totalling R2100 per device. Service is R50 per month for the first device plus R25 for each additional device on the same account. One device totals R400 per month for six months, then R50. Two total R775, then R75. Shipping and taxes must be disclosed before approval.

## PayPal in USD

Before consent, show the rand reference price, USD per rand rate, source and timestamp, rounding, each USD charge and billing dates. Explain any bank or PayPal conversion charges separately. Do not use a sandbox example rate for live billing. The live conversion policy remains to be approved.

## Automatic monthly billing

Proposed structure: one finite six-cycle device subscription and a separate ongoing service subscription, both explicitly approved by the customer. Device charges stop after six cycles; service continues until cancellation under the final terms.

Recommended service start is activation, not pre-order entry. Decide the first device instalment date separately, at dispatch or another clearly disclosed date. Do not collect a combined device/service amount while usable service start is undefined.

## Delivery and delays

Insert the approved dispatch window, courier, shipping price and transit estimate by region. Manufacturing time and courier time are separate. Send tracking after handover. Notify customers promptly of delays and offer the applicable cancellation and refund options.

## Cancellation before dispatch

Proposed policy: full refund of amounts paid for undelivered goods and unprovided service when cancelled before dispatch, subject to final approval. Subscription cancellation stops future billing but does not automatically refund past payments. Confirm both actions. Do not impose an automatic outstanding-device-balance charge without an approved contract and applicable credit-law assessment.

## Online cooling off

Where ECTA section 44 applies, buyers generally have seven days after receiving goods to cancel without reason or penalty; direct return costs may be allocated as permitted by law. Qualifying refunds are due within 30 days of cancellation. Services and statutory exclusions need separate assessment. Do not publish a blanket no-refunds clause.

## Defects and returns

Where CPA sections 55 and 56 apply, qualifying goods that fail the required standards may be returned within six months of delivery at supplier risk and expense without penalty. Statutory repair, replacement or refund remedies apply. A courier claim must not displace consumer rights against the seller.

Customers should contact support with their order reference and problem description. Give a case reference and approved return route. Do not request SOS history. Ask customers to stop using or charging swollen, damaged or overheating devices and obtain safe return instructions.

## Refund handling

Use the original payment channel where possible and record USD paid and refunded. Bank or PayPal conversion can change the rand amount credited. Do not promise an exchange-rate outcome outside seller control. Confirm refund initiation and processing expectations while preserving mandatory deadlines.

## Complete before publication

Confirm legal seller/address; dispatch window; shipping rates; return address; first charge and activation dates; cancellation obligations; device ownership; USD conversion policy; tax treatment; support availability and tested claims. Obtain South African review of the instalment and subscription arrangement before issuing final contracts.
