# Feasibility Study — Circuit Forge Technologies

**Project:** Electronics Engineering Services Company  
**Location:** South Africa  
**Parent Company:** [Skyline IT](https://skylineit.site/)  
**Date:** June 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Circuit Forge Technologies proposes to establish a specialized electronics engineering services firm in South Africa, targeting the growing demand for custom PCB design, embedded systems development, Internet Of Things (IoT) solutions, and hardware prototyping across African markets. The study confirms technical, operational, and financial feasibility with a clear path to profitability within 12-18 months.

**Key Findings:**
- Strong market demand with limited local competition in the SME/startup segment
- Low initial capital requirement relative to potential revenue
- Clear differentiation through local support and rapid turnaround
- Positive ROI projected by end of Year 1

## 2. Market Feasibility

### 2.1 Market Size & Growth

| Segment | Estimated African Market Size | Growth Rate |

| PCB Design Services | $120M (2026) | 11.2% CAGR |
| Embedded Systems | $280M (2026) | 14.5% CAGR |

### 2.2 Target Market Segments

1. **Startups & Entrepreneurs** — MVPs, proof-of-concept builds, small-run production
2. **SMEs** — Industrial automation, monitoring systems, custom control solutions
3. **Educational Institutions** — Training kits, lab equipment, STEM programs
4. **Agriculture Sector** — Smart farming sensors, irrigation controllers, monitoring
5. **Security Industry** — Access control, alarm systems, surveillance controllers
6. **Research Organizations** — Custom instrumentation, data acquisition systems

### 2.3 Competitive Analysis

| Competitor Type | Strengths | Weaknesses | Our Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| International firms (China, EU) | Lower PCB prices | Long lead times, no local support | Local support, faster turnaround |
| Local hobbyist freelancers | Low cost | Inconsistent quality, no scale | Professional standards, reliability |
| Large consultancies | Deep expertise | High cost, enterprise focus | Affordable, startup-friendly |
| In-house R&D teams | Domain knowledge | High overhead, slow | Cost-effective, specialized |

### 2.4 Demand Indicators

- 200+ hardware startup incubators active across Africa
- Growing maker movement with 45+ active maker spaces in SA alone
- Increasing Internet Of Things (IoT) adoption in agriculture, mining, and logistics
- Government initiatives supporting local manufacturing and tech innovation
- Shortage of local PCB design and embedded systems expertise

## 3. Technical Feasibility

### 3.1 Required Technology & Tools

| Category | Tools | Status |
|---|---|---|
| PCB Design | KiCad, Altium Designer, Eagle | Available (open-source + licensed) |
| Firmware Dev | VS Code, PlatformIO, STM32CubeIDE, Arduino IDE | Available |
| Simulation | LTSpice, Proteus, MATLAB/Simulink | Available |
| Prototyping | Soldering station, oscilloscope, logic analyzer, multimeter, power supplies | Capital investment required |
| Testing | Signal generator, spectrum analyzer, thermal camera | Phased investment |

### 3.2 Skills Required

| Skill | Availability | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PCB Layout (2-4 layer) | In-house | Founder + contract |
| Embedded C/C++ | In-house | Founder |
| Python/MicroPython | In-house | Founder |
| Internet Of Things (IoT) Protocols (Wi-Fi, BLE, LoRa, MQTT) | In-house | Founder |
| 3D CAD / Enclosure Design | Contract | Freelance partnership |
| Regulatory Compliance (CE, FCC, ICASA) | Contract | External consultant |
| RF Design | Contract | External consultant |

### 3.3 Infrastructure Requirements

- **Workspace:** Home office for initial phase (0-6 months), then co-working/lab space
- **Lab Equipment:** Initial R50,000-80,000 investment for essential tools
- **Software:** Mix of open-source (KiCad, FreeRTOS) and licensed (Altium if needed)
- **Cloud:** Git, project management tool, cloud backups, website hosting

**Verdict:** Technically feasible with low initial barrier to entry. Complex RF and compliance work can be subcontracted initially.

## 6. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low initial client volume | High | High | Pre-launch marketing, partnership building, parent company referrals |
| Cash flow constraints | Medium | High | Start lean, phased equipment purchases, invoice factoring |
| Late payments from clients | Medium | Medium | 50% upfront deposit, clear payment terms, contracts |
| Technology changes | Low | Medium | Continuous learning, modular tool stack |
| Competition from overseas | Medium | Low | Differentiate on local support, speed, and communication |
| Staff turnover | Low | Medium | Knowledge documentation, competitive pay, growth opportunities |
| Supply chain delays | Medium | Medium | Maintain buffer stock, multiple supplier relationships |

## 7. Legal & Regulatory Feasibility

### 7.1 Business Structure

- **Type:** Private Company (Pty) Ltd
- **Registration:** CIPC registered
- **Tax:** VAT registration recommended from Month 6
- **BEE:** Consider EME/QSE status based on revenue

### 7.2 Required Registrations

| Registration | Required | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| CIPC Company Registration | Yes | Pre-launch |
| Tax Clearance (SARS) | Yes | Pre-launch |
| VAT Registration | Yes | Month 6 (or earlier) |
| UIF/SDL/COIDA | Yes | Pre-launch (first employee) |
| ICASA (if RF products) | Yes | Per project basis |
| Professional Indemnity Insurance | Recommended | Month 1 |

### 7.3 Intellectual Property

- All client IP remains property of client (work-for-hire)
- Circuit Forge retains right to use general knowledge and methodologies
- Consider registering company trademark
- NDA template for client discussions
