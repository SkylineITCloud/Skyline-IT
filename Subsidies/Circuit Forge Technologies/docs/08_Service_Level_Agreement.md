# Service Level Agreement (SLA) — Circuit Forge Technologies

---

## 1. Scope

This Service Level Agreement (SLA) outlines the service standards, response times, and responsibilities for clients engaging Circuit Forge Technologies for electronics engineering services.

**Valid for:** All project-based and retainer engagements  
**Last Updated:** June 2026

---

## 2. Service Definitions

### 2.1 Service Categories

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **PCB Design Services** | Schematic capture, PCB layout, Gerber generation, BOM |
| **Firmware Development** | Embedded software for MCUs, testing, documentation |
| **Internet Of Things (IoT) Solutions** | Full-stack Internet Of Things (IoT) development (hardware + cloud + app) |
| **Hardware Prototyping** | Physical prototype builds, testing, iteration |
| **Consulting** | Technical advisory, architecture review, design review |
| **Support & Maintenance** | Bug fixes, updates, technical support after delivery |

### 2.2 Support Tiers

| Tier | Description | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | Email support, 24-48hr response | Business hours (Mon-Fri, 8am-5pm SAST) |
| **Priority** | Email + phone, 4-8hr response | Business hours |
| **Critical** | Phone + on-site (if local), 1-2hr response | 24/7 (for retainer clients) |

## 3. Response & Resolution Times

| Issue Severity | Standard | Priority | Critical |

### 3.2 Resolution Time Targets

| Severity | Target Resolution |
|---|---|
| Critical | Within 8 business hours |
| High | Within 2 business days |
| Medium | Within 5 business days |

---

## 6. Client Responsibilities

To ensure SLA compliance, the client agrees to:

1. Provide clear and complete requirements before project start
2. Respond to design reviews within 3 business days
3. Provide timely feedback and approvals
4. Make decision-makers available for milestone reviews
5. Pay invoices within NET-7 terms
6. Provide test environments or access where needed
7. Report issues with clear reproduction steps

## 8. Service Credits

If Circuit Forge Technologies fails to meet SLA response times, the following credits apply:

| Missed SLA | Credit |
|---|---|
| Response time exceeded by 2x | 5% off next invoice |
| Response time exceeded by 4x | 10% off next invoice |
| Critical issue unresolved for 24hrs | 15% off next invoice |
| Repeated failures (3+ in a quarter) | 20% off next invoice |

> Credits do not apply to delays caused by client late responses, incomplete requirements, or force majeure.
