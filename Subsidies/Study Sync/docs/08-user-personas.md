# User Personas & Target Audience: StudySync

**Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Primary Persona: The Collaborative Learner

### Persona: Alex Chen

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Age** | 20 |
| **Education** | 2nd-year Computer Science, University of California |
| **Study Style** | Pomodoro technique, group discussions |
| **Tech Proficiency** | High |
| **Device** | MacBook + iPhone |

**Bio:** Alex is a motivated CS student who struggles with procrastination when studying alone. They had a study group in freshman year that dissolved after one person dropped out. They've tried Discord servers but find them distracting. Alex wants a structured, focused way to study with peers who are equally committed.

**Goals:**
- Find reliable study partners in the same courses
- Maintain a consistent study schedule
- Track progress and stay motivated
- Reduce procrastination through accountability

**Pain Points:**
- Hard to find classmates with the same availability
- Generic chat apps are full of distractions
- Loses motivation studying alone
- No easy way to coordinate study schedules

**Behavior:**
- Studies 2-3 hours daily, prefers afternoon sessions
- Active on Reddit (r/csMajors) and Discord
- Checks Instagram/TikTok during breaks
- Willing to pay $3-5/mo for a tool that genuinely helps

**How StudySync Helps:**
- Matches with CS students on Pomodoro schedule
- Built-in timer eliminates context-switching
- Streaks and leaderboards provide motivation
- Shared calendar makes scheduling effortless

## 2. Primary Persona: The Social Studier

### Persona: Maya Rodriguez

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Age** | 19 |
| **Education** | 1st-year Biology, University of Texas |
| **Study Style** | Group quizzing, active recall |
| **Tech Proficiency** | Medium |
| **Device** | Windows laptop + Android phone |

**Bio:** Maya is outgoing and learns best through discussion. She feels lost in large lectures and wants a small group to review material with. She tried forming a WhatsApp group but it devolved into social chat. She wants study-focused accountability.

**Goals:**
- Form a small, committed study group for biology
- Review lecture material through group discussion
- Prepare for exams together
- Make friends while studying

**Pain Points:**
- Large classes make it hard to connect with peers
- Study groups she's tried lack structure
- Easily gets off-topic without a timer or agenda
- Doesn't know how to find serious study partners

**Behavior:**
- Studies 1-2 hours daily, prefers evening sessions
- Active on TikTok and Instagram
- Shares study content on social media
- Would recommend good tools to friends

**How StudySync Helps:**
- Connects with biology students using active recall
- Group chat with shout-outs builds camaraderie
- Task tracker keeps everyone accountable
- Daily streaks make studying a habit

## 3. Secondary Persona: The Exam Crammer

### Persona: Jordan Kim

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Age** | 22 |
| **Education** | Final-year Engineering, Georgia Tech |
| **Study Style** | Intensive sprints, flashcards |
| **Tech Proficiency** | High |
| **Device** | MacBook + iPad |

**Bio:** Jordan is a high-achieving senior who studies intensively during exam periods. They're looking for short-term, intense study groups for specific exams. They need efficiency and minimal fluff.

**Goals:**
- Find exam-specific study groups (FE exam prep)
- Intense, focused study sprints (2-4 hours)
- Access to shared resources and problem-solving
- Maximize study efficiency

**Pain Points:**
- Existing groups are too casual for exam prep
- Loses time coordinating instead of studying
- Needs peers at the same academic level
- Doesn't want long-term commitment

**Behavior:**
- Studies primarily during exam periods (3-4 weeks)
- Uses Pomodoro in 50/10 intervals
- Studies 6+ hours/day during exam season
- Researches tools extensively before trying

**How StudySync Helps:**
- Creates exam-specific groups with clear end dates
- Intense Pomodoro sessions with group members
- Task lists for exam topics to cover
- Leaderboard creates friendly competition

## 4. Secondary Persona: The International Student

### Persona: Yuki Tanaka

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Age** | 23 |
| **Education** | Master's in Economics, London School of Economics |
| **Study Style** | Flashcards, written notes, partner quizzing |
| **Tech Proficiency** | Medium |
| **Device** | Windows laptop |

**Bio:** Yuki is an international student adjusting to a new country and language. They want to find study partners but feel shy about reaching out. A structured, low-pressure way to connect would help them build both academic and social confidence.

**Goals:**
- Find study partners without the social pressure
- Improve English through academic discussion
- Understand course material through peer explanation
- Build a social network in a new country

**Pain Points:**
- Language barrier makes casual group formation hard
- Unfamiliar with local study culture
- Anxious about approaching strangers
- Needs inclusive, patient study environment

**Behavior:**
- Studies 3-4 hours daily, very disciplined
- Prefers text-based communication initially
- Relies on structured schedules
- Would value a safe, moderated environment

**How StudySync Helps:**
- Algorithm matches based on availability and method
- Text-first chat reduces language anxiety
- Stuctured sessions remove awkwardness
- Inclusive community with clear guidelines

## 5. Tertiary Persona: The Parent (Decision-Maker)

### Persona: Dr. Sarah Mitchell

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Age** | 52 |
| **Role** | Associate Dean of Academic Affairs |
| **Institution** | Large public university (30K+ students) |
| **Tech Proficiency** | Low-Medium |

**Bio:** Dr. Mitchell oversees student success initiatives. She's concerned about first-year retention rates and wants tools that help students build study skills and peer connections. She needs data, security assurance, and ease of adoption.

**Goals:**
- Improve first-year retention by 5%
- Provide accessible study support at scale
- Ensure student data privacy (FERPA compliance)
- Easy integration with existing LMS

**Pain Points:**
- Students aren't using existing support services
- No visibility into peer study engagement
- Privacy concerns with third-party platforms
- Budget constraints for new tools

**How StudySync Helps:**
- Institutional dashboard shows engagement metrics
- FERPA-compliant architecture
- No student data sold or shared
- Affordable per-student licensing ($2-5/year)
