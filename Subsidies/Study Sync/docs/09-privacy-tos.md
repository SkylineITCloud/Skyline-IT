# PRIVACY POLICY

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Data:** Name, university email address, password (hashed), course/program, study method preference, weekly availability
- **Profile Data:** Study preferences, course information
- **User Content:** Chat messages, task descriptions, group names, session names

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, session duration, Pomodoro completions, tasks completed, group interactions
- **Device Data:** Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
- **Analytics:** Feature usage patterns, engagement metrics (anonymized)

### 1.3 Information We DO NOT Collect
- Real names (beyond what you provide), phone numbers, physical addresses
- Social security numbers, government IDs, financial account numbers
- Grades, transcripts, or academic records
- Location data (beyond general timezone)
- Biometric data

## 2. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Legal Basis (GDPR) |
|---------|-------------------|
| Provide and maintain the service | Contract performance |
| Match you with compatible study groups | Legitimate interest |
| Send notifications (sessions, messages, tasks) | Contract performance |
| Improve and optimize the platform | Legitimate interest |
| Analyze usage patterns for product development | Consent |
| Communicate about service changes | Contract performance |
| Prevent abuse and enforce terms | Legitimate interest |

## 3. Data Sharing

### 3.1 We Share With:
- **Group Members:** Your name, course, study preferences, and availability are visible to group members
- **Service Providers:** Hosting provider (for server infrastructure), email service (for notifications)
- **Legal Authorities:** If required by law or to protect rights

### 3.2 We Do NOT Share With:
- Third-party advertisers (we have no advertising model)
- Data brokers or analytics companies (beyond anonymized, aggregated metrics)
- Social media platforms (no social login trackers)

## 4. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| Account data | Until account deletion | Service delivery |
| Chat messages | Until account or group deletion | Service delivery |
| Session history | 2 years after last activity | Analytics improvement |
| Pomodoro records | 1 year after last activity | Feature optimization |
| Backup data | 30 days after deletion | Disaster recovery |

## 5. Your Rights

### 5.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)
- **Right to Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure:** Delete your account and all data
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Export your data in JSON format
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing for legitimate interests
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time

### 5.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale of personal information
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising rights

### 5.3 Exercising Your Rights
Contact **privacy@studysync.app** with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

## 6. Data Security

- Passwords hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds)
- JWT tokens with configurable expiry
- All data transmitted over HTTPS
- Parameterized database queries prevent injection
- Regular security audits and dependency scanning
- No plaintext storage of sensitive information

## 7. Cookies

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|--------|---------|----------|------|
| `ss_token` | JWT authentication token | 7 days | Essential |
| `ss_user` | Cached user data | 7 days | Essential |

We use no tracking, advertising, or third-party cookies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

StudySync is designed for users aged 18 and older (university students). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR).

## 9. International Data Transfers

Data is stored on servers in the United States. For EU users, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as the transfer mechanism.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of material changes via email and in-app notification at least 14 days before changes take effect.

## 11. Contact

- **Privacy Concerns:** privacy@studysync.app
- **Data Protection Officer:** dpo@studysync.app
- **Physical Address:** [To be registered]

# DMCA TAKEDOWN POLICY

## Filing a Takedown Notice

If you believe content on StudySync infringes your copyright, send a notice to **dmca@studysync.app** containing:

1. Your physical or electronic signature
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
3. Identification of the infringing material and its location on the service
4. Your contact information (address, phone, email)
5. A statement of good faith belief that the use is not authorized
6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate

## Counter-Notice

If you believe content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notice with the same contact information, including a statement of consent to jurisdiction.
