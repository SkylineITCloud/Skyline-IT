## Slide 2: The Problem

**Headline:** Studying alone is lonely, distracting, and ineffective

**Key Points:**
- 60%+ of university students study primarily alone
- 73% report feeling isolated in their academic work
- Generic platforms (Discord, WhatsApp) are too distracting
- Passive tools (Quizlet, Chegg) don't provide accountability
- No solution exists that combines **group matching + structured study tools + accountability**

**Visual:** Split screen — left shows student alone/distracted, right shows engaged group studying

**Data:** "Students who study in groups retain 90% more material (National Training Laboratories)"

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

**Headline:** StudySync — Purpose-built for collaborative studying

**Key Points:**
- **Smart matching** — Algorithm pairs students by course, method, and availability
- **All-in-one** — Pomodoro timer, task tracker, shared calendar, group chat
- **Accountability** — Streaks, leaderboards, badges keep students motivated
- **Privacy-first** — No ads, no data selling, minimal data collection

**Visual:** Product screenshots in a grid (4 features)
