## 7. Financial Assumptions

- 50% upfront deposit required on all projects
- Average project payment terms: 30 days from invoice
- Component costs at 15-20% of project value
- 30% of projects require outsourced PCB fabrication
- Pricing increases of 10% from Month 7 onward
- No debt financing — operations funded from revenue
- Founder salary increases in Phase 2 as revenue scales

---

## 8. Contingency Plan

| Scenario | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue 30% below target | Cash flow constrained | Reduce marketing, increase consulting hours, delay hiring |
| Revenue 30% above target | Capacity constrained | Hire freelance support earlier, invest in automation |
| Major client default | Short-term cash gap | Line of credit from Skyline IT, invoice factoring |
| Economic downturn | Reduced spending | Focus on essential services, education sector, government contracts |
