# Business Plan — Circuit Forge Technologies

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Circuit Forge Technologies is a South African electronics engineering company offering professional PCB design, embedded systems development, Internet Of Things (IoT) solutions, and hardware prototyping. As a subsidiary of Skyline IT, we leverage existing business infrastructure while operating as a specialized hardware engineering division. Our mission is to make professional electronics design accessible to African innovators.

## 4. Market Analysis

### 4.1 Target Market

- Hardware startups and entrepreneurs seeking MVP development
- SMEs requiring custom automation and monitoring solutions
- Educational institutions needing training equipment and kits
- Agricultural technology companies
- Security system integrators
- Research organizations

### 4.2 Competitive Advantage

- **Local support:** Face-to-face consultation, same timezone
- **Rapid turnaround:** 2-3x faster than international alternatives
- **Cost-effective:** 40-60% less than European/US firms
- **End-to-end:** Single vendor from concept to production
- **Parent backing:** Skyline IT infrastructure and referrals

## 5. Marketing & Sales Strategy

### 5.1 Channels

- Direct outreach to hardware startups (incubators, accelerators)
- Partnership with maker spaces and university engineering departments
- Online presence (website, LinkedIn, GitHub portfolio)
- Referral program through Skyline IT network
- Attendance at tech events and hackathons
- Google Ads targeting "PCB design South Africa" keywords

### 5.2 Sales Process

```
Lead Generation → Discovery Call → Technical Assessment
    → Proposal → Deposit (50%) → Project Kickoff
    → Milestone Reviews → Final Delivery → Payment (50%)
    → Support Period
```

## 6. SWOT Analysis

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Specialized technical expertise | Limited brand awareness (new entrant) |
| Low overhead, lean operation | Single founder dependency (initially) |
| Parent company support & referrals | Limited lab equipment initially |
| Local presence, rapid turnaround | No track record / portfolio |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---|
| Growing African hardware ecosystem | International competition (China/EU) |
| Internet Of Things (IoT) adoption across all industries | Economic downturns affecting spending |
| Government manufacturing initiatives | Rapid technology changes |
| Underserved SME/startup market | Talent retention challenges |
