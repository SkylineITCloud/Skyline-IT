## 1. Brand Positioning

## 2. Target Personas

### Persona A: Hardware Startup Founder
- **Needs:** MVP development, quick turnaround, limited budget
- **Pain points:** Can't afford overseas firms, local options lack quality
- **Channel:** LinkedIn, incubator programs, referrals
- **Offer:** Startup package — discounted PCB + firmware bundle

### Persona B: SME Operations Manager
- **Needs:** Custom automation, monitoring systems, process control
- **Pain points:** Off-the-shelf solutions don't fit, needs customization
- **Channel:** Direct outreach, industry events, referrals
- **Offer:** Free consultation and feasibility assessment

### Persona C: University Lecturer/Researcher
- **Needs:** Custom lab equipment, training kits, research prototypes
- **Pain points:** Budget constraints, complex specifications
- **Channel:** Academic partnerships, conference networking
- **Offer:** Educational discount (15%), collaboration opportunities

### Persona D: Agritech Entrepreneur
- **Needs:** Sensor systems, Internet Of Things (IoT) monitoring, irrigation control
- **Pain points:** Need rugged, field-ready solutions
- **Channel:** Agricultural tech events, online targeting
- **Offer:** Pilot program with discounted first unit
