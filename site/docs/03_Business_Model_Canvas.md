## 1. Value Propositions

- **Professional PCB design** — Industry-standard designs with rapid turnaround
- **Embedded systems expertise** — ESP32, STM32, ARM, RPi, PIC
- **End-to-end hardware development** — From concept to production support
- **Local support** — South African-based, face-to-face consultation
- **Cost-effective solutions** — 40-60% less than international firms
- **Internet Of Things (IoT) specialization** — Smart devices, monitoring, automation
- **Educational focus** — Training kits, STEM resources, university partnerships

---

## 2. Customer Segments

- Hardware startups and entrepreneurs
- SMEs (manufacturing, agriculture, security)
- Educational institutions (universities, technical colleges)
- Research organizations
- Agricultural technology companies
- System integrators
- Individual makers and inventors

## 3. Channels

- **Direct sales** — Email, phone, in-person meetings

- **GitHub** — Open-source contributions, portfolio showcase

## 4. Customer Relationships

- **Personal consultation** — Free initial discovery call

- **Regular updates** — Weekly progress reports

- **Retainers** — Ongoing maintenance and support contracts

- **Community building** — Makers community, newsletter
