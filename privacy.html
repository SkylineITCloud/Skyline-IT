Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 July 2026

1. Who We Are

Skyline IT & Cloud ("Skyline IT", "we", "us", "our") is a South African holding company operating across digital services, Internet Of Things (IoT) products, electronics engineering, and education technology. Our subsidiaries and divisions include Skyline IT & Cloud (digital services), Mzansi Connect (Internet Of Things (IoT) products), Circuit Forge Technologies (electronics engineering), StudySync (EdTech), and VALOW. (client brand). We are based in Durban, South Africa.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us. When you contact us through our website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name (optional)
  • The service you are interested in
  • Any message content you send us

We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we do not require any information beyond what is needed to respond to your enquiry.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and quotations
  • Match your request to the correct division or subsidiary
  • Provide, manage, and support our services
  • Keep you informed about your project (with your consent)

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. Our legal basis for processing is your consent, which you give when submitting your details, and the legitimate business interest of responding to your enquiry.

5. Sharing of Information

We only share your information within the Skyline IT group where necessary to fulfil your request (for example, passing an Internet Of Things (IoT) enquiry to Mzansi Connect or an electronics enquiry to Circuit Forge Technologies). We do not share your information with any unrelated third parties, except where required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against loss, theft, unauthorised access, or disclosure. Our websites are hosted on secure platforms (GitHub Pages and cloud infrastructure managed by Skyline IT & Cloud).

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website does not use intrusive tracking cookies. Any analytics we use are aggregated and do not identify individuals.

8. Your Rights Under POPIA

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Third-Party Services

Some of our products use third-party platforms (such as Supabase for database services and hosted analytics). These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. Where we control the data, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

10. Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, contact us at projectpstg@gmail.com or via post at Durban, South Africa.
